Interactive Bubble Game

OpenCV + MediaPipe + Pygame

A hand-controlled game inspired by Wii Fit's Balance Bubble game.

src="https://youtu.be/FRq_-fIp0VA"

Features

Stream
Hand-controlled input
Finish Line

Our game includes a background of a stream. The player tries to navigate to the finish line while avoiding obstacles (rocks) and walls (land). Our game is unique as it utilizes OpenCV and MediaPipe to get user input. If the player wants to go right, they close their right hand, and to go left, they close their left.
